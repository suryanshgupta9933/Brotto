Brotto drives this browser

Let you ask an AI to perform tasks in your own logged-in browser.

You describe a task in your own words. Brotto works through it and tells you when it needs a decision from you.

Working
Browsing, reading, filling in forms. You can keep using other tabs.
Needs you
An approval, a sign-in, or a question. Brotto stops and waits — you decide.
Done
The answer, with what it did and what it found.
