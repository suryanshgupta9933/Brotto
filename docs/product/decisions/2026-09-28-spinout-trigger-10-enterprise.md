# Decision: Vertical spinout trigger — 10 enterprise customers in same vertical

**Date:** 2026-09-28
**Question:** Q5 (open-questions.md)
**Status:** Accepted

## Context

Four trigger options:
- $5M ARR + clear vertical pattern
- $2M ARR + clear vertical pattern
- 10 paying enterprise customers in same vertical
- Defer entirely

User chose: 10 paying enterprise customers in same vertical.

## Choice

**Trigger vertical spinout at 10 paying enterprise customers in the same vertical.**

Why this trigger:
- ARR is vanity if the customers aren't vertically aligned.
- Enterprise customers pay real money; their willingness-to-pay validates the workflow.
- "10 in same vertical" is unambiguous signal, not noisy pattern in free tier.
- Forces discipline: we don't spin out until real revenue from real buyers in one vertical.
- Aligns with C1 (generalist first): wait for the vertical to emerge from usage, don't impose it.

## Consequences

**Enabled:**
- Data-driven vertical decision (vs. bet-driven)
- Spinout product has paying customers from day 1, not just usage data
- Lower risk of building the wrong vertical product

**Closed off:**
- No spinout until 10 enterprise customers land in the same vertical
- Could take 12+ months to reach this trigger from launch
- Requires enterprise sales motion to exist (currently doesn't)

**New constraints:**
- Enterprise sales motion is a Phase 4–5 deliverable (currently planned for Phase 5)
- Need a way to track "which vertical" each enterprise customer is in (CRM tagging, customer intake form)
- Spinout product needs a launch path from Brotto generalist (shared infra, separate marketing, separate pricing)
