# Decision: Funding — Post-launch on traction

**Date:** 2026-09-28
**Question:** Q6 (open-questions.md)
**Status:** Accepted

## Context

Three options:
- Pre-launch funding — raise before launch to fund Phase 1+2
- Post-launch on traction — raise after Phase 1+2 + early traction
- Bootstrap on ARR — no external funding

User chose: Post-launch on traction.

## Choice

**Raise after Phase 1+2 ship and we have traction signals: >1000 Pro users, >$5k MRR, >5k GitHub stars.**

Bootstrapping entirely would slow SOC2 + vertical + sales motion by 6–12 months. Pre-launch funding uses leverage before signal and dilutes before we've proven anything. Post-launch on traction is the cleanest: prove the wedge, then raise on it.

Target raise size: $2–5M seed (or Series A-equivalent) to fund SOC2 + sales motion + vertical. Comparable to Browser Use's $17M seed (raised on 110k stars) and Skyvern's $2.7M (raised later on enterprise traction).

## Consequences

**Enabled:**
- Founder optionality preserved until signal exists
- Valuation reflects real traction, not pre-revenue promise
- Raises on a story: "BSL-OSS Chrome extension, BYOK, $X MRR, Xk stars, Y enterprise customers"
- VC funders love OSS + traction (Browser Use / Skyvern precedent)
- Compatible with BSL license (commercial-grade license signals seriousness to VCs)

**Closed off:**
- Can't accelerate Phase 3+ (reliability, sales) with outside capital until traction
- SOC2 has to wait for funding
- Slow phases if revenue is slower than expected

**New constraints:**
- Bootstrap Phase 1+2 (auth, onboarding, CWS, OSS launch, first 1000 users)
- Track leading indicators of fundability: stars, MRR, Pro users, enterprise pilots
- Pick a target raise date (~6 months from Phase 2 launch?) and reverse-engineer what needs to be true
